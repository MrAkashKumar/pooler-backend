Reusable system

Product components

01

Match card

Identity, trust, route overlap and fair walking comparison before invitation.

02

Common Point card

Public landmark, pickup quality, Rider A/B ETA, distance and fairness delta.

03

Expiry status

Consistent countdown for invitations and chat, with explicit expired state.

04

Safety action

Block, report and stop sharing remain reachable during every active meetup.

05

External handoff

Large provider actions shown only after both riders confirm physical arrival.

Hoppo wireframe v2 Product states align with the Spring Boot discovery, invitation, Common Point, chat, Telegram, saved location and ride lifecycle modules.
